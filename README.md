# Features

## Login / Sign up Feature
Has a Login and Sign up Feature for Students, Teachers and Administrators.


## Admin
The administrator of this app and makes the Grades level, sections, and subjects and its percent to the student's grade.

## Teachers
Has the Ability to Grade and check the attendance of students registered to their advisory and chosen subject sections and make Activities and Quiz scores 

## Students
An easier way to check if you have missing activities and check when you've absentee
